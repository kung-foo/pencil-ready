---
title: "Multiplication"
summary: "Multiply multi-digit numbers with partial products, one row per digit of the multiplier."
prerequisites:
  - "Single-digit times tables, 0–10 at minimum."
  - "Column addition with carrying."
  - "Multiplication as repeated addition."
learning:
  - "Write one partial product per digit of the multiplier."
  - "Shift each partial product one place left as you move up the multiplier."
  - "Add the partial products to reach the final answer."
---
