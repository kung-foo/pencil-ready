---
title: "Prime factorization"
summary: "Write a number as a product of primes by drawing a factor tree: split it into two factors, and keep splitting until every branch ends in a prime."
prerequisites:
  - "Factor pairs: splitting a number into two factors."
  - "Knowing the primes up to about 50."
  - "Times-table fluency."
learning:
  - "Draw a factor tree and circle the prime at the end of each branch."
  - "Write the number as the product of those primes, smallest first: $24 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 3$."
  - "See that any first split gives the same primes in the end."
---
