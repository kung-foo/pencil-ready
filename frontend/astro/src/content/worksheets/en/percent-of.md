---
title: "Percent of a number"
summary: "Find a percent of a number, from 10% and 50% up to any percent with a decimal answer."
prerequisites:
  - "Percent as hundredths (see fractions, decimals and percents)."
  - "Dividing by 10, halving and quartering."
  - "Column multiplication, two digits by two digits."
learning:
  - "Find 10% by dividing by 10 and 50% by halving."
  - "Build other percents from those, or combine steps: $35% = 25% + 10%$."
  - "Multiply the percent by the number, then divide by 100 by moving the decimal point."
---
