---
title: "Percent of a number"
summary: "Find a percent of a number, from 10% and 50% up to percents built from smaller steps. Every answer is a whole number."
prerequisites:
  - "Percent as hundredths (see fractions, decimals and percents)."
  - "Dividing by 10, halving and quartering."
  - "Multiplying a whole number by a one-digit number."
learning:
  - "Find 10% by dividing by 10 and 50% by halving."
  - "Build other percents from those: 20% is two tens, 75% is three quarters."
  - "Combine steps for awkward percents: $35% = 25% + 10%$."
---
