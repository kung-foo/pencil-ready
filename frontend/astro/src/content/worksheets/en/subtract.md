---
title: "Subtraction"
summary: "Subtract the smaller number from the larger, borrowing when a digit is too small."
prerequisites:
  - "Column addition with carrying."
  - "Counting backwards, and addition as its inverse."
  - "Recognizing when the top digit is smaller than the bottom one."
learning:
  - "Line up numbers by place value and subtract column by column from the right."
  - "Borrow ten from the next column, crossing out as you go."
  - "Handle borrows that ripple across consecutive zeros — the hardest case."
---
