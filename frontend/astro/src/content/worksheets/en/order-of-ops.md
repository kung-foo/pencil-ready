---
title: "Order of operations"
summary: "Evaluate expressions mixing +, −, ×, and ÷, with parentheses overriding the usual order."
prerequisites:
  - "Single-digit addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division."
  - "Reading an expression from left to right."
  - "Arithmetic with two numbers at a time, before combining three."
learning:
  - "Apply precedence — × and ÷ before + and −."
  - "Work left to right within the same precedence group."
  - "Treat parentheses as forcing whatever is inside them to go first."
---
