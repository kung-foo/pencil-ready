---
title: "Squares and square roots"
summary: "Solve x² ± b = c and √x ± b = c for x, with both families mixed on one page."
prerequisites:
  - "Squares 0² to 10² and the matching roots, from memory."
  - "One-step and two-step equations."
  - "Squaring and taking a square root as inverse operations."
learning:
  - "Isolate the squared or rooted term, then undo it."
  - "Move in both directions between x² and √x on the same sheet."
  - "Read six forms — three built on x², three on √x."
