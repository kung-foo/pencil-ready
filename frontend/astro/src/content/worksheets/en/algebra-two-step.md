---
title: "Two-step equations"
summary: "Solve ax + b = c for x, undoing the constant first and then the coefficient."
prerequisites:
  - "Arithmetic facts for addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division."
  - "A letter as a placeholder for an unknown number."
  - "One-step equations, and inverse operations."
learning:
  - "Undo operations in reverse order — subtract the constant, then divide by the coefficient."
  - "Keep the equation balanced — do the same thing to both sides."
  - "Read three forms — (ax) + b = c, b + (ax) = c, and (ax) − b = c."
