---
title: "Fractions, decimals and percents"
summary: "Write one number three ways: as a fraction, a decimal and a percent, with one of them given."
prerequisites:
  - "Place value to hundredths (and thousandths for the top level)."
  - "Equivalent fractions: rewriting a fraction over 10, 100 or 1000."
  - "Simplifying fractions."
learning:
  - "Read a decimal as a fraction over 10, 100 or 1000, then simplify it."
  - "Turn a fraction into a decimal by rewriting it over 10, 100 or 1000."
  - "Move between decimals and percents: percent means hundredths."
