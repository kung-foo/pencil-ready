---
title: "Factor pairs"
summary: "Find every factor of a number as pairs that multiply to make it, trying 1, 2, 3 and up until the pairs meet."
prerequisites:
  - "Times-table fluency."
  - "Division with remainder, to test whether a number divides evenly."
  - "Knowing that multiplying in either order gives the same answer."
learning:
  - "Find every factor of a number without missing any, by working up from 1."
  - "Know when to stop: once the pairs meet, every factor has been found."
  - "Recognize a prime (only $1 × p$) and a square number (a pair with itself)."
---
