---
title: "Addition"
summary: "Add whole numbers in columns, carrying when a column sums past 9."
prerequisites:
  - "Counting reliably to at least 20."
  - "Place value for each digit — ones, tens, hundreds."
  - "The + symbol as combining two quantities."
learning:
  - "Line up numbers by place value and add column by column from the right."
  - "Carry the tens digit when a column sum reaches 10 or more."
  - "Check a result by re-reading the carried digits in each column."
---
