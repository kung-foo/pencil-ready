---
title: "Division drill"
summary: "Drill the times tables in reverse, one division fact per line."
prerequisites:
  - "Multiplication drill fluency for the same tables."
  - "Division as undoing multiplication."
  - "Multiplication facts to at least 10 × 10."
learning:
  - "Name the quotient for any product in a known table."
  - "See that one product carries two facts — 56 ÷ 7 and 56 ÷ 8."
  - "Recall quickly enough to make simple and long division routine."
---
