---
title: "Decimal addition"
summary: "Add decimal numbers in columns, lining up the decimal points."
prerequisites:
  - "Whole-number column addition with carrying."
  - "Reading a decimal as tenths and hundredths after the point."
  - "Place value on both sides of the decimal point."
learning:
  - "Line up the decimal points before adding anything."
  - "Carry across the decimal point exactly as in whole-number addition."
  - "Pad with trailing zeros so every number has the same decimal places."
