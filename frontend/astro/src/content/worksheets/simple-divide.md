---
title: "Division"
summary: "Recall times tables in reverse — given a product and one factor, find the other."
prerequisites:
  - "Multiplication facts to at least 10 × 10."
  - "Multiplication and division as inverse operations."
  - "Sharing a quantity into equal groups."
learning:
  - "Read a division as how many groups of one number fit inside another."
  - "Recall a quotient straight from a known times table."
  - "Work only with clean, evenly dividing cases — the groundwork for long division."
---
