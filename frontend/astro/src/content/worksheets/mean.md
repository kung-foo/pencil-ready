---
title: "Mean"
summary: "Find the mean of a set of numbers — add them up, then divide by how many there are."
prerequisites:
  - "Column addition with carrying, for three or four numbers at a time."
  - "Long division by a single-digit divisor."
  - "Reading a decimal to one tenth, for the longest sets."
learning:
  - "Work in order — total everything before dividing anything."
  - "Divide by how many values there are, not by a number from the set."
  - "Check the mean sits in the middle of the data, which catches most slips."
---
