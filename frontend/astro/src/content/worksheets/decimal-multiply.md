---
title: "Decimal multiplication"
summary: "Multiply a decimal by a whole number or by another decimal, then place the point."
prerequisites:
  - "Single-digit times tables."
  - "Whole-number column multiplication."
  - "Place value in a decimal — tenths and hundredths."
learning:
  - "Multiply as if both numbers were whole, then place the decimal point."
  - "Count decimal places — the answer has as many as both numbers together."
  - "Sanity-check the size — 2.5 × 3 lands near 7, not near 75 or 0.75."
