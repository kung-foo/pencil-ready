---
title: "One-step equations"
summary: "Solve for x in a single move, undoing one of +, −, ·, or ÷."
prerequisites:
  - "Arithmetic facts for the operations in use."
  - "A letter as a placeholder for an unknown number."
  - "Each operation paired with its inverse — addition with subtraction, multiplication with division."
learning:
  - "Isolate x by applying the inverse of the operation acting on it."
  - "Keep the equation balanced — do the same thing to both sides."
  - "Read four forms — x + b = c, x − b = c, a · x = c, and x ÷ a = c."
